Rank the terms of a sum from the biggest down, and fold the parameters of the index:

- a term keeps its sign; only the first line loses its operator, for the `=` that opens the expression
- without an evaluation there is nothing to rank by, and the author's order stands
- `RuleIndex` opens its parameters on the reader standing among them, and the results lose their label: they open the index

A rule whose value is written as a literal no longer offers an empty sheet, and `EquationView.note` goes with the sentence it carried. A page is projected once, and a sheet only where it prints: a 300-rule model goes from 754 ms to 1,2 ms.
