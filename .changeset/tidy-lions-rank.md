Rank the terms of a sum from the biggest down — without an evaluation, the author's order stands — and fold the parameters of `RuleIndex`, which open on the reader standing among them. The results lose their label: they open the index.

A rule whose value is written as a literal no longer offers an empty sheet, and `EquationView.note` goes with the sentence it carried. A page is projected once, and a sheet only where it prints: a 300-rule model goes from 754 ms to 1,2 ms.
